# Pricing

The Basic plan costs 29 EUR per month and includes 5 team members and 20 GB of storage.

The Pro plan costs 99 EUR per month and includes 25 team members, 200 GB of storage and priority support.

Enterprise pricing is custom and starts at 500 EUR per month with a dedicated account manager.

Annual billing gives a 20 percent discount on Basic and Pro plans.

Extra storage costs 5 EUR per 50 GB per month. Overage is billed monthly in arrears.
