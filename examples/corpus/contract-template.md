# Master Service Agreement Template

The customer pays invoices within 30 days of the invoice date.

Late payments accrue interest of 1.5 percent per month.

Either party may terminate the agreement with 60 days written notice.

The total liability of the provider is limited to the fees paid in the 12 months before the claim.

The agreement is governed by the laws of the Netherlands.

Confidentiality obligations survive termination for 3 years.
