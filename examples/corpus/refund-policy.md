# Refund Policy

Customers may return most items within 30 days of delivery for a full refund.

Electronics are refundable within 14 days of delivery and only with the original packaging.

Items must be unused and include all accessories. Opened software, gift cards and personalised goods are not refundable.

Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5 business days after the returned item passes inspection.

Shipping fees are refunded only when the return is caused by a defect or a wrong item. Return shipping for a change of mind costs 9 EUR and is deducted from the refund.
