# Support

Support works Monday to Friday from 09:00 to 18:00 Central European Time.

Channels are email, in-app chat and the customer portal. Phone support is available for Enterprise customers.

Priority 1 incidents receive a first response within 1 hour. Priority 2 within 4 business hours. Priority 3 within 2 business days.

A Priority 1 incident means the production service is unavailable for all users.

Support is provided in English and German.
