# Delivery Terms

Standard delivery takes 2 to 5 business days inside the EU and is free for orders above 75 EUR.

Orders below 75 EUR pay a flat delivery fee of 6 EUR.

Express delivery is available in selected cities and arrives within 24 hours for an extra 15 EUR.

We ship from the warehouse in Rotterdam. Orders placed before 14:00 local time are dispatched the same day.

Remote areas and islands may add 1 to 3 business days. We do not deliver to PO boxes.
