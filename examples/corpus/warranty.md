# Warranty

All hardware products carry a 24 month warranty from the delivery date.

The warranty covers manufacturing defects and battery capacity below 80 percent within the first 12 months.

The warranty does not cover accidental damage, water damage, unauthorised repairs or normal wear of consumable parts.

Customers must provide the order number and a description of the fault. Repairs take up to 20 business days.

If a defect repeats three times within the warranty period, the customer may request a replacement unit.
