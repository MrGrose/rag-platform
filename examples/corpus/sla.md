# Service Level Agreement

The service targets 99.9 percent monthly uptime for the API.

If monthly uptime falls below 99.9 percent, the customer receives a 10 percent service credit.

If monthly uptime falls below 99.0 percent, the credit increases to 25 percent.

Credits must be requested within 30 days after the affected month and are applied to the next invoice.

Planned maintenance is announced 72 hours in advance and is excluded from the uptime calculation.
