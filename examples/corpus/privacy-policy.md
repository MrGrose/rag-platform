# Privacy Policy

We collect account data, order history and support messages to provide the service.

Personal data is stored for 24 months after the last activity, then anonymised.

Payment card data is processed by the payment provider and never stored on our servers.

Customers may request export or deletion of their data by writing to privacy@northwind.example.

We use a data processor in the EU and do not transfer personal data outside the European Economic Area.

The Data Protection Officer can be reached at dpo@northwind.example.
